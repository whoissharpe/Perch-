# Attribution

Perch shows third-party data and photographs. Both carry licence conditions,
and both are conditions rather than courtesies.

## Base map

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed ODbL. Shown in-app on the map
attribution control, which must stay legible - see `apps/mobile/src/components`.

## Perch Picks photographs

The curated picks in `apps/mobile/src/curated.ts` are real places. Coordinates
come from each place's Wikipedia record; photographs are freely licensed images
from Wikimedia Commons. Credit is rendered over the photo on the card and again
on the spot detail screen, alongside a link back to the source record.
