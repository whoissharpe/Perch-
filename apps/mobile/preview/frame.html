Perch — iPhone preview

The real app running at iPhone size (390 × 844). Everything is live: pan and zoom the map, tap the birds, switch tabs.

Things to try

  1. Zoom in past street level. Each bird swoops, beats its wings twice and lands on a bench. Zoom back out and the bench goes.
  2. Tap a bird. It turns to face you with wings spread and its post opens below.
  3. Tap it again to fold its wings and put it back on the bench.
  4. Hollow rings are spots seeded from OpenStreetMap that nobody has photographed yet.
  5. The clay + is the capture flow — it asks you to sign in first.

Jump the map

The landing only plays when the zoom crosses 15.5 — that is the point where a bench is legible.

Note

This is the web build of the same React Native code, so the map is MapLibre rather than the native map. On a device it is Apple/Google Maps with the same pine styling. Location shows as LOCATION OFF unless you allow it in the browser.
